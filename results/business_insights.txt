======================================================================
NETFLIX DATA ANALYSIS - BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
======================================================================
Analyzed Dataset : data/Dataset(1).csv
Total Titles     : 8,790 records

1. Content Type Breakdown (Movies vs. TV Shows):
   Movies represent 6,126 titles (69.69% of the library),
   while TV Shows represent 2,664 titles (30.31%).
   In this dataset, movies outnumber TV shows by more than 2 to 1.

2. Leading Content Producing Countries:
   After splitting multiple country listings individually, the United States
   has the highest number of titles with 3,240 entries,
   followed by India with 1,057 titles and the United Kingdom
   with 638 titles. India is the largest Asian content
   provider in the dataset.

3. Complete Content Release Year Trend:
   The dataset contains content originally released between 1925 and 2021.
   Across this entire period, the year 2018 has the single highest number of releases
   with 1,146 titles, followed by 2017 (1,030 titles) and 2019 (1,030 titles).
   Most content in the catalog was released between 2015 and 2020.

4. Target Audience Age Ratings:
   The two most frequent ratings are TV-MA (3,205 titles, 36.46%)
   and TV-14 (2,157 titles, 24.54%).
   Together, TV-MA and TV-14 make up 61.00% of all titles,
   showing that the vast majority of Netflix's library is cataloged for teen and mature adult audiences.

5. Top Content Genres:
   When counting each individual genre, "International Movies" (2,752 appearances),
   "Dramas" (2,426 appearances), and "Comedies" (1,674 appearances)
   are the top three genres. "International TV Shows" ranks fourth (1,349 appearances),
   demonstrating that international titles form a major component of both movies and TV shows.

6. Missing Data and Metadata Observations:
   In the raw dataset, missing entries were stored with placeholder text 'Not Given'.
   Specifically, 2,588 titles (29.44%) have no listed director,
   and 287 titles (3.27%) have no country listed.
   In contrast, title, content type, release year, rating, and duration have 100% complete records.
